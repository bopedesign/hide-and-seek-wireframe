Canton, Georgia, 18th summer

A safe, loving place to spend the summer outdoors

Hide and Seek Day Camp is a Christ rooted day camp for kindergarten through fifth grade, open to every family. Six weeks of summer, three campuses, and counselors who know your child by name.

Kindergarten through 5th grade, plus one middle school week

When

June and July, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Where

Hickory Flat, Ball Ground, and Sixes

How long we have run

18 summers of camp in Cherokee County

What a day at camp feels like

Mornings start with snacks and assembly. The rest of the day is rotations through the woods, the field, and the craft tables, with Bible teaching woven through all of it. Thursday is water day. Friday the woods are open.

The Camp Experience

Outdoors most of the day, in small groups, with counselors who care. Here is what a week actually looks like.

The daily rhythm

7:30 to 9 a.m. Drop off
9:15 to 9:45 a.m. Snacks
9:45 to 10:15 a.m. Assembly
10:15 to 11:45 a.m. Three rotations of curriculum, arts and crafts, field games, gaga ball, and free choice
11:45 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. Lunch
12:15 to 2:45 p.m. Four to five rotations
2:45 to 3 p.m. Carpool
3 to 6 p.m. Late pick up

Thursday is water day

Slip and slide, relay races, and water games. Send a towel and a change of clothes.

Friday is open woods

Free reign of the outdoor activities to close out the week.

Bible teaching

Curriculum is one of the rotations, but the teaching is not confined to it. Counselors talk about the attributes of God on walks around camp and campers memorize scripture that flows from the week's lesson.

Safety

Parents leave their children with us so they can go to work. We take that seriously. Here is exactly how we screen and train the people who spend the day with your camper.

Photo: counselor helping a camper on the ropes course

Background checks

Every Hide and Seek employee receives a background check before the summer begins.

References

All employees provide several references and complete counselor training.

Ropes course training

Staff are trained on the ropes course by a certified third party before camp starts, and several are certified operators.

CPR and first aid

Camp directors are CPR and first aid trained.

Day to day

Small groups

Campers stay with one group and one counselor all week.

Carpool

Named pick up authorization at every campus.

Weather

Indoor space at all three campuses and a rainy day drop off plan.
