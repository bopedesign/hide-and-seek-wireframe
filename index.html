The basics

Where is Hide and Seek Day Camp?

Camp runs at three campuses. Hickory Flat is at Mt. Zion Baptist Church, 4096 East Cherokee Drive, Canton. Ball Ground is at Ball Ground Methodist Church, 3045 Canton Hwy, Ball Ground. Sixes is at 8385 Bells Ferry Road, Canton. Our mail is received at a different address, 1894 Lower Union Hill Rd, Canton, GA 30115.

What are the camp hours?

Our regular camp program begins at 9 a.m. and ends at 3 p.m. Pre camp supervision is provided starting at 7:30 a.m. and post camp supervision is provided until 6 p.m. each day.

What is the price of camp?

The price of camp is organized by the session, meaning the week the camper attends. You can register for as many sessions as you like. Sessions are $275 and run 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Early drop off is 7:30 a.m. to 9 a.m. for $15 per session. Post camp is 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. for $30 per session. To register for pre or post camp, your camper must be registered for the regular hours of camp. They are add on options, not individual session hours.

Will camp still run during holiday weeks?

Yes. Our most common conflicts are Memorial Day and the Fourth of July. We will not have camp on either of those days, but every other day of that week camp runs as usual. A four day session is priced lower than a full five day session.

Can I get help paying for camp?

Yes. Scholarships are available at every campus and the application is short.

Getting ready for the day

What should my child wear to camp?

Any comfortable and modest clothing is acceptable. Closed toe shoes are required for most activities. Camp attire requirements are that no undergarments are showing, including sports bras. Please mark your child's first and last names on all belongings.

What should my child bring each day?

A sack lunch, a snack, closed toe shoes, a refillable water bottle, sunscreen, and a swimsuit and towel on Thursdays. Please mark your child's first and last names on all belongings.

At camp

Is water provided on hot days?

Yes, water is provided every day. Water coolers are located at stations throughout the campus. Campers typically carry their water bottles with them, and our staff will make stops for water between activities to make sure your child is hydrated at all times.

How will my child be placed in a group?

Children are grouped according to grade level, age, and gender, and can also be placed according to their group request form. If you wish to request a group or counselor, you can complete that form during registration. All groups are supervised by a counselor. If a group exceeds a certain number of campers, a junior counselor is assigned to help manage the larger group.

What if it rains?

Camp will be in session as usual. We have lots of fun and special activities just for those rainy days.

If my child is having a birthday, will you do something special?

Of course. Please let us know and we will recognize any camper with a birthday during one of our assemblies.

Are there any off campus field trips?

No, everything takes place on campus. We do occasionally have guest speakers who come in and provide fun activities for our campers.

Do we have to be a church family?

No. Camp is Christ rooted and open to everyone.

Drop off and pick up

How does drop off and pick up work?

Weather permitting at Hickory Flat, drop off is in the back parking lot of Mt. Zion Baptist Church near the overhang. If drop off needs to move due to inclement weather, it moves inside the chapel and is accessed via the side door. The pick up line begins at the side door of the chapel and wraps around the back of the chapel. A staff member will approach your car and call for your camper, who will be sent to the carpool line. Please move all the way up the line so we can serve as many cars as possible. Only those on your camper's pick up list will be allowed to pick up your child. Cell phone use is not permitted in the carpool line.

Directions for Ball Ground and Sixes are on each campus page.

Our staff

What is the staff selection process?

Staff members apply through our online application in late winter. New applicants are interviewed individually by the director. Once the applicant passes that interview, they participate in a group interview with the other counselor applicants and submit a background check. Once hired, staff attend training in May covering low and high ropes training, first aid, curriculum, games, and small group activities.

Are your staff members background checked?

Yes. All Hide and Seek Day Camp employees receive a background check prior to the start of the summer. All employees are required to provide several references and go through counselor training. All employees also receive ropes course training from a certified third party corporation before the start of the summer. Your child's safety is our highest priority.

Camp leadership

Director

Monday to Friday, hours vary · Education background and experience required

We are looking for a leader who is a hard worker and has a big heart for interacting with children and their families. Directors lead spiritual growth in both counselors and campers, monitor all camp activities, supervise staff, handle camper check in and check out, and facilitate carpool. The director handles weekly scheduling and group assignments for campers and staff, and ensures the safety and well being of the campus throughout the summer.

Program Director

Monday to Friday, 7:15 a.m. to 4 p.m. · Age 18+ · Camp or education experience preferred

Leads daily assemblies and games at camp. Sets up and takes down ropes course equipment and supervises ropes course activities each day. Assists with drop off and carpool and sets up materials for the daily program. Works closely with the campus director to keep programming running smoothly.

Adventure leads

Hickory Flat only. Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Age 18+, camp or education experience preferred. Every adventure lead serves and supervises counselors and junior counselors, reports to the program director, and sets up, takes down, and facilitates the ropes course during the day. Each also leads a small group of counselors one day a week and attends a small group of their own one day a week.

Curriculum Emphasis

Sets up and cleans up materials for Bible curriculum time, introduces the curriculum during morning assembly, and guides groups through the curriculum rotation. Leads groups as small as ten campers and as large as the full camp of roughly one hundred twenty campers and counselors.

Camp Emphasis

Leads age based camp adventure activities during the adventure rotation, such as how to set up a tent and how to tie knots. Occasionally leads camp wide activities of around one hundred twenty campers and counselors.

Games Emphasis

Creates and facilitates games and activities during each games rotation. Regularly leads groups of fifty campers and counselors, and occasionally leads camp wide activities of around one hundred twenty people.

Specialty leads

Photography Lead

All locations · Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. · Age 18+ · Photography experience required

Posts regularly on the camp social media pages and creates camp promotion material, highlighting camp and campers. Also leads a small group of counselors one day a week and attends a small group of their own one day a week.

Arts and Crafts Lead

Monday to Thursday, 9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. · Age 18+

A leadership counselor position that involves planning, preparing, and creating craft projects for campers. May lead groups of up to thirty campers at one time with their counselors.

Counselors

Counselor

Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. · Age 17+

Counselors lead groups of up to eight campers through scheduled camp activities throughout the day, including facilitating the zip line, belaying campers at the rock wall, art and games, and teaching curriculum. Counselors must be comfortable teaching a daily Bible curriculum provided to them, and must pass a ropes course facilitation training program to be hired on staff.

Junior Counselor

Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. · Age 16+

Junior counselors assist with day to day activities. They help counselors with group activities, monitor children during morning drop off and afternoon pick up, and participate in assemblies.

Post camp

Post Camp Lead

Monday to Friday, 2:30 p.m. to 6 p.m. · Age 18+

Transitions campers into post camp activities, supervises campers after camp hours, interacts with parents during pick up, and monitors post camp activities. Comes in early to set up all supplies for the day and cleans up with the other counselors at the end.

Post Camp Counselor

Monday to Friday, 2:30 p.m. to 6 p.m. · Age 16+

Supervises children after camp hours, leads games, and stays engaged with campers during their time after camp.

Training and screening

Every employee completes a background check, provides several references, and goes through counselor training before campers arrive. Ropes course training is delivered by a certified third party.
